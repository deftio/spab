# spab benchmark

spab 0.5.1, wire format v2. Generated by `node r_and_d/benchmark.js`.

Research measurement, not a gate. `tests/` proves the implementation works;
this measures how well, and where it does not. Every number moves when the
codec moves — that is what they are for.

## 0. What is being tested

Every channel model, what it does, and the real situation it stands in for.
A result is only readable if you know what produced it, so this table comes
before any number.

`control` must always survive — a failure there is a bug, not a measurement.
`channel` is ordinary document handling. `attack` is someone deliberately
removing a mark, which spab explicitly does not claim to resist.

## 1. Capacity

What a document can hold before robustness enters. `bytes/copy` is the largest
incompressible payload that fits once, so it already nets off packet overhead.

| sample           | chars | words | default | ws-only | punct | wsdense | bytes/copy |
|------------------|------:|------:|--------:|--------:|------:|--------:|-----------:|
| memo             |   390 |    61 |     120 |     120 |     5 |     180 |          9 |
| punctuation-rich |   307 |    42 |      82 |      82 |    13 |     123 |          5 |
| punctuation-poor |   295 |    57 |     112 |     112 |     0 |     168 |          8 |
| short-lines      |   188 |    35 |      54 |      54 |     0 |      81 |          1 |
| markdown         |   200 |    32 |      52 |      52 |     5 |      78 |          1 |
| code-ish         |   213 |    28 |      44 |      44 |     1 |      66 |          0 |
| long-prose       |  1144 |   224 |     446 |     446 |     4 |     669 |         50 |
| tiny             |    35 |     7 |      12 |      12 |     0 |      18 |          0 |
| no-spaces        |    43 |     1 |       0 |       0 |     0 |       0 |          0 |
| pre-marked       |   198 |    35 |      68 |      68 |     1 |     102 |          3 |

Capacity columns are bits at one copy for the STRONGEST channel in that set,
not a sum. Carrier classes are independent parallel channels and each carries
the whole payload, so what decides whether a payload fits is the largest single
channel — which is why `default` (ws + apos + hyphen) matches `ws-only`. The
extra classes buy failure diversity, not room.

`punct` is small on purpose: it is the channel that survives NFKC, and prose
simply does not contain many apostrophes and hyphens.

## 3. Real-world channels, by redundancy

Recovery per corruption model at its median intensity, **split by how many
copies of the payload fit**. Redundancy is the dominant variable and a single
averaged column hides it: the same model reads 0% on a passage that fits one
copy and 100% on one that fits eight. Quoting the average of those would
describe neither.

Each sample is therefore measured at several lengths (1x, 3x, 8x, 20x) and the
results are grouped by the redundancy actually achieved.

## 5. Safety

The invariant the project actually promises. A mark may be lost; it must never
come back as something else, and unmarked text must never yield a payload.

| measurement                         |     result | requirement            |
|-------------------------------------|-----------:|------------------------|
| payload reported from unmarked text |    0 / 100 | must be 0              |
| wrong payload from a damaged mark   |   0 / 1904 | must be 0              |
| recovered at worst-case intensity   | 1062 (56%) | reported, not required |
| lost at worst-case intensity        |  842 (44%) | reported, not required |

## 6. Cost and distortion

`distortion` is the share of eligible carrier sites the encoder actually
changed — the steganalysis-relevant number, since an untouched site looks like
every other untouched site.

| carriers   | note                           | encode | decode | distortion |     length |
|------------|--------------------------------|-------:|-------:|-----------:|-----------:|
| default    | ws + apos + hyphen             |      - |      - |          - |          - |
| ws-only    | whitespace alone               |      - |      - |          - |          - |
| punct      | NFKC-durable only              |      - |      - |          - |          - |
| wsdense    | 8 variants, 3 bits/site        |      - |      - |          - |          - |
| zero-width | inserts, not length-preserving | 0.03ms | 0.02ms |       556% | +204 chars |
